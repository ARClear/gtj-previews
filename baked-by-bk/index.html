How ordering works

There's no checkout here. Every order starts as a request, so Brandy can make sure she has room in her schedule to bake it right.

  1. Send a request

    Tell Brandy what you'd like, how many, and the date you need it. Please allow at least one week's notice.

  2. Brandy confirms

    She checks her schedule and replies with a price. Nothing is booked until you say yes.

  3. Pick up in Blue Springs

    Orders are pickup only from Brandy's home in Blue Springs, MO. She'll send the address when she confirms.

Brandy bakes for anyone in the Kansas City metro who's happy to make the drive to Blue Springs. She doesn't ship or deliver.
